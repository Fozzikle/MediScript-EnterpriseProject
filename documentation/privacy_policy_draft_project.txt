MediScript Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 4 july, 2025
Last Updated: 3 july 2025

MediScript values your privacy and is committed to transparency about how the application operates with regard to your data.

Data Collection: MediScript does not collect, transmit, or store any user data, audio recordings, transcripts, or personal information. All files generated by the application are stored locally on your device, in a location that you specify during setup.

No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not utilize third-party analytics, telemetry, or tracking services. No data is sent to MediScript Pty Ltd or any affiliated third party.

Your Responsibility: You are solely responsible for managing, securing, and deleting your files. If you are handling health information, you must comply with applicable privacy and health records legislation, including but not limited to the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and State-based Health Records Acts.

Changes to Policy: MediScript reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be communicated through official release notes or the company website.

For inquiries, contact mediscript_support@team.mediscript.com.au
